Urdu words and their Hindi equivalents

26 words in Urdu, each in the script with a roman reading, the Hindi meaning and the English meaning. Tap Listen to hear it, where your device has a Urdu voice.

In the street, Hindi and Urdu are one language. A speaker in Lucknow and a speaker in Bhopal say the same sentences with the same grammar. The two part ways in higher vocabulary: for formal and learned words, Urdu turns to Persian and Arabic, and Hindi turns to Sanskrit. So a book is a किताब in Urdu and everyday speech, and a पुस्तक in formal Hindi.

Most Indians use both words of each pair without noticing, choosing by mood and occasion. "वक़्त क्या हुआ है?" is what you ask a friend; "समय" is what the station announcer says. Knowing the pairs makes you at home in both registers, and makes news, speeches and poetry in either language much easier to follow.

Notes

  • Neither word of a pair is more "correct". Both belong to the shared speech of North India. Good speakers choose by setting: the Sanskrit-based word in a formal Hindi speech, the Persian or Arabic word in Urdu writing, and whichever comes naturally in conversation.
  • The dotted Devanagari letters क़ ख़ ग़ ज़ फ़ were added precisely to write these Urdu sounds in Hindi. Our guide the five nuqta sounds explains each.
  • Some shades differ. उस्ताद is a master of a craft or an art as well as a teacher; ज़बान is both the tongue in the mouth and a language.
