Urdu words you know from songs and poetry

26 words in Urdu, each in the script with a roman reading, the Hindi meaning and the English meaning. Tap Listen to hear it, where your device has a Urdu voice.

Even people who say they know no Urdu know hundreds of Urdu words, because Hindi film songs, ghazals and qawwalis are full of them. दिल, ज़िंदगी, ख़्वाब, इंतज़ार, तन्हाई: you have sung these words all your life. This page shows how they are written and exactly what they mean.

Why does poetry prefer these words? Partly for sound: Urdu's Persian and Arabic words are rich in long vowels and soft consonants that sing well. Partly for tradition: the ghazal came to India through Persian, and its stock of images, the moon, the candle, the journey, the wound, came with it. And partly for shades of meaning. इंतज़ार is not just waiting but waiting with longing. आरज़ू is not just a wish but a deep yearning.

This page lists single words and their meanings only. It quotes no song or poem. To read real Urdu poetry, look for editions of the classical poets, whose work is long out of copyright.

How these words combine

Urdu poetry joins two words with a short "e", called izafat, to mean "of": دردِ دل (dard-e-dil), "the pain of the heart"; شامِ غم (shaam-e-gham), "the evening of sorrow"; جانِ من (jaan-e-man), "life of mine". Two words can also be joined with "o", meaning "and": شب و روز (shab-o-roz), "night and day". Once you know these two tiny joints, half the titles of ghazals open up to you.

Some poetry terms: a شعر (sher) is a couplet, شاعری (shayari) is poetry, a شاعر (shayar) is a poet, a غزل (ghazal) is a poem of linked couplets, and a مشاعرہ (mushaira) is a gathering where poets recite.
