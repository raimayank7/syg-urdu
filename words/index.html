You know more Urdu than you think

Every Hindi speaker uses hundreds of Urdu words daily: दिल, दोस्त, किताब, वक़्त, ज़िंदगी, मुश्किल, आसान, ज़रूर, शायद. Film songs, ghazals, news bulletins and courtrooms add hundreds more. These lists take the words you already own and show you how they are written, where they come from and what shade of meaning they carry.

The lists

  • Words of songs and poetry: the vocabulary of the ghazal and the film song, with exact meanings.
  • Urdu and Hindi word pairs: the Persian or Arabic word beside its Sanskrit-based twin.
  • Numbers: the number words and the Urdu digit shapes.
  • Family and days and time, including the day names that differ from Hindi.
  • Everyday words and verbs: plain, familiar words chosen for reading practice.

How to use a word list for reading practice

Each card has four lines: Urdu, roman, Devanagari with a gloss, English. Hide all but the first. Sound the word out from the right, supply the short vowels, and say it. Because these are words you know, you will feel the moment when the letters turn into a word. That moment, repeated a few hundred times, is what reading is. The Words mode of the practice quiz will test you on all of them.
