Family

22 words in Urdu, each in the script with a roman reading, the Hindi meaning and the English meaning. Tap Listen to hear it, where your device has a Urdu voice.

Urdu names relatives with the same precision as Hindi, and in most homes with the very same words: भाई, बहन, बेटा, बेटी, दादा, नानी, चाचा, मामा. Alongside these it has a formal set from Arabic and Persian. Father is वालिद, mother वालिदा, parents वालिदैन. In affectionate daily speech they are अब्बू or अब्बा, and अम्मी.

Two words differ from the usual Hindi ones and are worth learning. Mother's sister is خالہ (khala), where Hindi says मौसी; her husband is ख़ालू. Father's sister is پھوپھی (phuphi), where Hindi says बुआ. Mother's brother is ماموں (mamoon), close to the Hindi मामा.

Respect is added with صاحب (sahib) for men and صاحبہ (sahiba) for women, placed after the word: वालिद साहब, भाई साहब, वालिदा साहिबा. Elders are spoken of in the plural, as in Hindi: "अब्बू आ गए हैं", not "आ गया है".

Notes

  • The small mark ّ over the ب in ابّو and the م in امّی is the tashdeed. It doubles the consonant: ab-bu, am-mi. It is often left out in print. See vowel marks.
  • بھ in بھائی is one sound, bh (भ), written as ب plus the "two-eyed h" ھ. All the breathy sounds of Hindi are written this way.
  • مہمان (guest) gives مہمان نوازی (mehmaan nawazi), hospitality, a quality Urdu-speaking culture prizes highly.
