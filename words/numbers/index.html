Urdu numbers and numerals

23 words in Urdu, each in the script with a roman reading, the Hindi meaning and the English meaning. Tap Listen to hear it, where your device has a Urdu voice.

Spoken Urdu numbers are the same as Hindi numbers: ek, do, teen, chaar. Nothing new to learn there. What is new is the writing, in two ways: the number words are written in Urdu letters, and Urdu has its own shapes for the digits.

Digit0123456789
Urdu۰۱۲۳۴۵۶۷۸۹
Devanagari०१२३४५६७८९

Here is a point that confuses every beginner: Urdu text runs right to left, but numbers inside it run left to right. The number 125 is written ۱۲۵, with the hundreds digit on the left, just as in English. Your eye has to change direction for a moment in the middle of a line.

In India, most printed Urdu today, including newspapers, uses the ordinary international digits (1, 2, 3). The Urdu digit shapes are still seen in books, calendars, on older signs and in handwriting, so they are worth knowing.

Notes

  • Zero is صفر (sifar) in Urdu. It is the Arabic word from which the English "cipher" and "zero" descend, and it is itself a translation of the Sanskrit शून्य.
  • The digits ۴ (4), ۶ (6) and ۷ (7) have different shapes in Urdu and Persian print from those used in Arabic. Fonts and keyboards treat them as separate characters.
  • Money: روپیہ (rupiya) rupee, روپے (rupaye) rupees, پیسہ (paisa). Indian banknotes carry the value in Urdu among the languages printed on the back.
  • Dates are read left to right inside the line: ۱۵ اگست ۱۹۴۷ is 15 August 1947.
