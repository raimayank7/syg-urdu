Introducing yourself

14 phrases in Urdu, each in the script with a roman reading, the Hindi meaning and the English meaning. Tap Listen to hear it, where your device has a Urdu voice.

An introduction in Urdu uses sentences that any Hindi speaker can already say. The point of this page is to see them written. Read each line from the right, letter by letter, with the Devanagari line below as your key. Within a few pages, common words such as میں (main, I), ہے (hai, is), کیا (kya, what) and آپ (aap, you) will be recognised as whole shapes.

Urdu also has its own formal vocabulary for these moments. Where everyday speech says "आपका नाम क्या है?", refined Urdu may ask "आपका इस्म-ए-शरीफ़?", literally "your noble name?". A student is a तालिब-ए-इल्म, "a seeker of knowledge". You do not have to use these forms, but you will hear and read them, and they are part of the pleasure of the language.

The little "e" that joins two words in such phrases is called izafat. It means "of" and is written with a small mark under the first word, or not written at all. Our Urdu and Hindi guide explains it.

  • میرا نام امن ہے

    mera naam Aman hai

    मेरा नाम अमन है

    My name is Aman

  • آپ کا نام کیا ہے؟

    aap ka naam kya hai?

    आपका नाम क्या है?

    What is your name?

  • آپ کا اسمِ شریف؟

    aap ka ism-e-sharif?

    आपका इस्म-ए-शरीफ़? (आपका शुभ नाम?)

    May I know your good name? (very polite)

  • میں ہندوستان سے ہوں

    main Hindustan se hoon

    मैं हिंदुस्तान से हूँ

    I am from India

  • آپ کہاں سے ہیں؟

    aap kahan se hain?

    आप कहाँ से हैं?

    Where are you from?

  • میں لدھیانہ میں رہتا ہوں

    main Ludhiana mein rehta hoon

    मैं लुधियाना में रहता हूँ

    I live in Ludhiana (said by a man)

  • میں طالب علم ہوں

    main talib-e-ilm hoon

    मैं तालिब-ए-इल्म (विद्यार्थी) हूँ

    I am a student

  • میں دفتر میں کام کرتی ہوں

    main daftar mein kaam karti hoon

    मैं दफ़्तर में काम करती हूँ

    I work in an office (said by a woman)

  • آپ کیا کرتے ہیں؟

    aap kya karte hain?

    आप क्या करते हैं?

    What do you do?

  • میری عمر بیس سال ہے

    meri umr bees saal hai

    मेरी उम्र बीस साल है

    I am twenty years old

  • میں اردو سیکھ رہا ہوں

    main Urdu seekh raha hoon

    मैं उर्दू सीख रहा हूँ

    I am learning Urdu

  • مجھے تھوڑی اردو آتی ہے

    mujhe thodi Urdu aati hai

    मुझे थोड़ी उर्दू आती है

    I know a little Urdu

  • یہ میرے دوست ہیں

    ye mere dost hain

    ये मेरे दोस्त हैं

    This is my friend (respectful)

  • آپ سے مل کر بہت اچھا لگا

    aap se mil kar bahut achchha laga

    आपसे मिलकर बहुत अच्छा लगा

    It was very nice to meet you

Reading practice on this page

Find these five small words in the cards and learn their shapes: میں main (I; also "in"), ہوں hoon (am), ہے hai (is), ہیں hain (are), سے se (from). They appear in nearly every Urdu sentence. Notice that "main" (I) and "mein" (in) are spelt the same, میں; the sentence tells you which is meant.
