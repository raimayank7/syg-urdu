Two kinds of learner, one page

These pages serve two kinds of reader. If you speak Hindi or Urdu and want to learn the script, read the Urdu line from right to left and use the Devanagari line below it as your answer key. You will understand every sentence already, which leaves your whole mind free for the letters. If you are new to the language, use the roman reading to say the phrase and the English line for its meaning.

What makes Urdu sound like Urdu

The grammar is the grammar of everyday Hindustani. The colour comes from vocabulary and manners: शुक्रिया rather than धन्यवाद, ख़ुश आमदीद for welcome, तशरीफ़ रखिए for "please sit". The page on polite words and tehzeeb collects the expressions that give the language its famous courtesy, with the exact sense of each.

How to study a phrase page

  1. Read the short introduction; it points out the letters and words to watch for on that page.
  2. Cover the helper lines and read each Urdu line aloud, slowly.
  3. Uncover and check. Mark the ones you got wrong and read them again tomorrow.
  4. Copy five phrases by hand. Writing fixes the shapes in a way that reading alone does not.

The Listen button reads the Urdu aloud where your device has an Urdu voice. Many devices do not; the roman line is then your guide.
